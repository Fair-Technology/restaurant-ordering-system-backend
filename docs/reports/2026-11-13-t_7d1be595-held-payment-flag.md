## What `paymentHeld` means and why the state guard is needed

`paymentHeld` means the card is still held and we are still retrying the capture. That is only true while the order is `PLACED`.
`applyTransition` (`src/domain/order/orderLifecycle.ts`) does **not** clear `captureConfigFailure` when the order
moves to `REJECTED` (the timeout path declines it with `payment_unavailable`). An unguarded `!!order.captureConfigFailure`
would therefore keep reporting "held" after the decline, when the authorization has already been released. The third
customer-order test guards against this regression.

We are looking into the problem.`

## Deviation from the brief

`buildEscalationEmail` picks its language from the **shop** (`menuLanguagesOf(shop)[0]`), not from `order.language`. With
`CARD_SHOP` (`menuLanguages: ['de']`), the briefed English case `{ ...PLACED_CARD_ORDER, language: 'en', … }` would still
render German, so the test would have failed. The EN test therefore calls `buildOrderEmail` with
`shop: { ...CARD_SHOP, menuLanguages: ['en'] }` and also keeps `language: 'en'` on the order. The language-selection
behaviour itself was not changed.
